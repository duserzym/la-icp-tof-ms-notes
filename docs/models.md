# Interactive models

Both viewers are single self-contained HTML files with Three.js r160 bundled (MIT license, see [Threejs-LICENSE.txt](figures/Threejs-LICENSE.txt)). They need a WebGL-capable browser and make no network requests. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. All geometry is schematic and every assumption is stated inside the viewer; see [Instrument illustrations](15-instrument-illustrations.md) for the full list.

## Instrument viewer

Mass separation in a folded ToF path, reflectron energy focusing (three tracer ions or a noisy many-ion ensemble), the H₂/He collision/reaction cell, the timed argon blanker and the MagneTOF detector.

[Open full screen](figures/Instrument-3D.html){ .md-button }

## Laser interaction and ablation-cell viewer

Absorption, spontaneous emission and stimulated emission, each paired with where it matters (sample surface, plume, laser cavity), plus a two-volume ablation cell with computed gas and particle paths (from [cell_flow_model.py](figures/cell_flow_model.py)), a conventional-cell comparison and a tubing-delay calculator.
