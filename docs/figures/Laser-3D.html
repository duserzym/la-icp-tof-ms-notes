What this model is and is not

The atom views use a Bohr-style cartoon with two energy levels and wave packets drawn in false color: a 193 nm photon is ultraviolet and invisible. The instrument views are schematic. The cell view illustrates the two-volume concept (a small, fast-swept ablation volume inside a large sample chamber); it is not manufacturer CAD of the TwoVol3. Its gas and particle paths are computed for that schematic geometry by cell_flow_model.py (steady laminar He on a staggered grid, particle tracking with Stokes drag); see note 02 for the assumptions.

Sources: Einstein coefficients, e.g. HyperPhysics: quantum processes; two-volume cell with integrated dual concentric injector, Loughborough repository; review of low-dispersion cells, critical evaluation (open PDF); vendor description of TwoVol3 with dual concentric injector, instrument facility page.
